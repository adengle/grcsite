# Great Rivers Cycling Community  
**Independent community resources for riders who enjoy the group rides that start at Trek O’Fallon**

Welcome to the Great Rivers Cycling Community page.  
This is an **independent, rider‑driven resource** created to support local cyclists, strengthen community connections, and make it easier for new and returning riders to join the fun.

This site is **not affiliated with Trek Bicycle Corporation** or the Trek O’Fallon store.  
We simply love riding bikes — and we love the community that gathers there.

## Group Rides

### Saturday Morning Road Ride
- **Rollout:** 8:00 AM  
- **Location:** Trek Bicycle – O’Fallon, MO  
- **Pace Groups:** A, B, C (no‑drop for C group)

## About This Site

This page is maintained by Great Rivers Cycling, a local rider‑driven initiative.  
It exists to support the O’Fallon cycling community by providing:

- Clear, predictable communication  
- Easy access to routes and resources  
- A welcoming on‑ramp for new riders  
- A hub for virtual and in‑person riding  

This is an **independent, non‑commercial, rider‑driven project** and is not affiliated with Trek Bicycle Corporation.
